# Main Plot Plus
[![Check Files](https://github.com/ziproot/endless-sky-main-plot-plus/actions/workflows/check-files.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/ziproot/endless-sky-main-plot-plus/actions/workflows/check-files.yml)

A plugin for the video game “endless sky” mostly centered around improving the main plot.

## Guided Start
This is an optional feature that can be enabled or disabled upon creating a new save or loading an existing one with this plugin installed for the first time. If you haven't chosen sides in the human civil war by July 3017, Guided Start will allow you to join the Free Worlds, and also the Syndicate if you have the Crisis in Management plugin installed. It will additionally allow you to remain neutral (see below). Any conditions blocking the campaigns from starting will be cleared. If you enter the spaceport, a map indicator will lead you to the planet to get started and remind you to enter the spaceport if necessary. You may choose to dismiss this for 30 days as many times as you want if you decide to do other tasks first.

## Fast Forward
This is a start two years later than the present. To unlock it, you must have made it to the point where Fast Forward begins and visited Hope and the Quarg. It automatically reveals all of human space and puts you in Wayfarer with no loan and enough credits to buy a Blackbird. Since it is two years later, you can immediately start doing the free worlds prologue missions, but you are also unable to do pact recon.

## Main Plot Skip
You can choose to skip the main plot. This is mostly useful for debugigng purposes, as this is not a full-fledged “merchant campaign.” To do this, first, enable the main plot skip. Next, just play the game normally without choosing sides, then when asked what side you are on, say that you are neutral. When pressed, continue to remain neutral. Shortly after, land on any world one jump away from a Pug planet and tell the Pug that you are neutral, and you will be able to skip the main plot. However, there's a catch: a different pilot will do the free worlds campaign instead, and they will choose checkmate. If you want the reconciliation ending, join the Syndicate with the Crisis in Management plugin or join the Free Worlds and choose reconciliation. The missions are designed so that you will be relocated to Ruin and time will advance by several years, but this is waiting on several pull requests to be merged, so at the moment you will have to use your imagination. I do give you a minor reward: a subsidurial pet.

### Compatibility
The main plot skip should be compatible with most major missions in the base game. You are told about the Pug war, the Free Worlds having nuclear weapons, and some of the new outfits produced by humanity: the plasma turret, flamethrower, s-970 regenerator, s-270 regenerator, ionic afterburner, typhoon torpedo launcher, electron beam, and catalytic ramscoop. This isn't everything, as it excludes the stack core due to it not being sold by Kraz Cybernetics, but it is enough to cover everything in the Coalition intros. You also learn about the Oathkeepers, and you assist Danforth by taking out a pirate fleet, unlocking the Wanderers campaign. From there, you cannot start Hai Reveal through Fiona and Rain, since Hai Reveal was removed from the game. I have additionally patched a mission that assumes you know Alondo.

## Road to Hai Reveal
Hai Reveal is still in a work in progress state. As of the latest update, none of it is in the game. If I find the time, I am considering adding in some version of Hai Reveal to the game, but as of now, Hai Reveal is still disabled, even with Main Plot Plus. I have also patched all the post-war reactions, see below. I have kept all Road to Hai Reveal missions in disabled form here, in case a version of Hai Reveal is added which is still Free Worlds dependent. If a version is added that is more campaign agnostic, or there is ever confirmation that Hai Reveal will never be added in any form, I will completely remove these missions from the game files. Events will still be kept due to the potential presence of old save files.

### Post War Reactions
The post-war reactions by default all assume you have directly fought with the Pug, which isn't necessarily true in Crisis in Management and definitely isn't true if you skip the main plot. I have replaced all three of these with patch missions which vary depending on how you interacted with the Pug, based on this plugin, the vanilla Free Worlds Reconciliation branch, and Crisis in Management. It also tries to test if you have done the vanilla version of Free Worlds Checkmate instead, if needed. I also removed a stupid section about the Free Worlds and Navy captains joking about almost killing each other, and I have reverted the gender of a character in War Reactions: Syndicate Orphan because MasterofGrey says that the change was not justified. Other than that, doing Free Worlds should result in the same post-war reactions as vanilla, but doing Crisis in Management or the Main Plot Skip option will give you different outcomes that make sense for what you've done.

## Syndicate
The Syndicate will now use their new technology on their ships if you complete the main plot, though they still won't sell it to you if you did reconciliation. The weights of the variants are based on the ones for the republic Mark II ships. A few of the Mark II variants are used in vanilla for a few missions in the reconciliation branch, but I added a bunch more so that all the Syndicate ships used in their main fleets have Mark II variants. The Syndicate rarely uses nuclear missiles before the war in the eastern parts of the Core, where they get raided by the Korath. They stop in 3014 because of the impending war.

## Rebuild Geminus
There’s an old plugin by Amazinite called “Enigma Corp” which had a short mission chain where you helped rebuild Geminus after you completed the main plot. This mission chain would end with you receiving the Navy license. As the Navy campaign is still not out, I have added this mission chain to Main Plot Plus. To start it, simply complete the main plot, then go to the spaceport on Geminus. This mission chain is optional in three ways. First, you can completely disable the possibility of this mission from offering via JamesGPT. Second, you can decline the first mission in the chain, or simply not visit the Geminus spaceport, and wait for Geminus to be rebuilt as normal. If the Navy campaign releases, I will remove this mission chain.

### Test Dummies
Do you know those test dummy missions that you have to do? Well, there's a reason for them. Amazinite's Checkmate improvements include “automatic” ships. I have added these ships to occasionally show up in the Syndicate fleets after the main plot. They are essentially the same as the normal “mark II” ships, except that they have a special government that can change hails to use the test dummy ones, they cannot be bribed, they have the same confusion as the target practice jobs, they have the “automaton” and “self-destruct” attributes from the jobs, they are always called “Syndicate Test Vessel,” and, being expensive, try to avoid battle and run away if threatened. Speaking of the test dummies, you can no longer fight them after the main plot, as the Syndicate has moved to the next phase of their testing and has started to incorporate them in their fleet.

## Improved Checkmate
Amazinite had, a long time ago, added changes that improved the Checkmate branch, that ultimately didn't go anywhere, other than some changes in the first part of the campaign rework. These changes will likely be added as part of the next parts of the campaign rework. However, as I am a fan of the Checkmate branch already, I think more people should be doing it, so I am incorporating these changes now, along with some minor fixes. Mostly, these allow you to ask about progress made by the Syndicate in finding the terrorists, and eventually allow you to “fight” them with automatic ships after the Pug invasion. This updated plot is referenced in the Main Plot Skip option, mentioning the Syndicate finding the “terrorists” and the automatic vessels being used as cannon fodder.

### Nuclear Missile (Mark II)
I am also changing one major thing: the nuke launcher is three times smaller, but it now launches “mark II nuclear missiles,” which use the buffed nuclear missiles proposed by Azure3141 as a base. When these begin being sold, the Syndicate also begin using them instead of regular nukes in a new “Mark II Nuclear” loadout in the systems raided by the Korath. This Manta loadout has two nuclear launch bays holding four mark II missiles, and two sidewinder missile launchers holding 90 sidewinder missiles, the latter of which ensure that the Mantas use the “missile boat” AI, making them less likely to blow themselves up. The old nuclear missiles are 10x cheaper than the nuclear missiles in vanilla are, i.e. they cost 100,000 credits each instead of a million credits each. The nuclear launch bay costs 1 million credits, and each mark II nuke costs 150,000 credits. However, you can restock from any outfitter via 0.10.13’s system, and these nukes are immensely more powerful, as one hit can disable a ship’s systems for several seconds. There is not a storage container for nukes as each one takes up weapon and outfit space directly, which is the main reason why the nuclear launch bay takes up so little outfit space on its own. However, you can still add one ton nuclear installation chips to *load* additional nukes. This is required mainly so that the game can let you refill to whatever target you want without you having to manually rebuy the nukes yourself.

## Hidden Sky
Until the main plot is completed, all systems in the game will be marked hidden. After the main plot is completed, no systems in the game will be marked hidden. Systems that had hidden and not shrouded will be marked inaccessible until the requisite mission reveals them, so yes, this means that after the main plot, no shrouded systems will also have hidden. This does not change much other than enabling a few Jump Drive paths that were otherwise not possible. Several plugins are directly supported, notably Jibauni, Rumskib, Lost in Midnight, Midnight Scrapyard, and Naltok. Gyrrwr Colonization is automatically supported. The mission where the Syndicate give you a Jump Drive in Free Worlds Reconciliation has also been altered slightly so that it is possible to jump into Pug space, and if you are stuck in the Ember Waste with a Jump Drive but no ability to get a keystone, the Remnant will update your map so you can leave without needing one.

## Presets
Rather than having to manually choose whether or not to enable all optional features, you may choose between one of several presets, which choose the missions for you. Of course, if you want to, you may manually choose which features to support. The following presets are supported:

* Default preset: This preset is only visible if you have manually chosen settings before and agreed to make them the default. This allows you to essentially create a custom preset, but only one custom preset at a time is currently supported.
* Recommended preset: This preset enables settings I think will provide the most interesting Main Plot Plus experience. This will automatically enable guided start if you have not managed to join a side without guided start, though due to game limitations, this does not detect if you have joined a side already before installing Main Plot Plus. It will also automatically enable hidden sky and the navy license mission. However, nothing will be enabled if you have loaded a pre-existing save.
* Vanilla+ preset: This preset enables settings I think will provide the most interesting Main Plot Plus experience that does not alter game balance too much. Right now, the only difference between this and the recommended preset is that this disables Hidden Sky.
* Allow all preset: This preset enables all optional features (currently the Navy License mission, Main Plot Skip, Hidden Sky, Guided Start, Security Analyses, and Blueberry1vom1t’s Discount Paint feature).
* Deny all preset: This preset disables all optional features.

## Other Changes

### Other Free Worlds Changes
The longest Han Sizer Month job now gives a brief paragraph of flavor text, made by Zitchas, once you land on the final planet before heading to your destination.

### Deep Science Drones
0.10.5 added the sunder and mining drones, so I decided that it was time to allow you to get your own science drones! Valhalla and Asgard sell a drone variant of the aerie along with science drones, and a fleets consisting of an aerie with four science drones will travel around the Deep. Miners will also use the drone variant with mining drones to mine. The aerie drone variant is also occasionally used by the pirate fleet in road to hai reveal.

These science drones use special new outfits, the Ant Core, which is a compact radioisotope thermal generator, and the A75 Atomic Engines, which are compact atomic engines. These currently use placeholder assets. If anyone is willing to provide sprites for these, that would be greatly appreciated. Furthermore, Terminus exploration has been updated to require Deep science drones and accurately describe the layout of the updated science drones. It also now drastically reduces random pirate spawns in Terminus while the mission is active.

### Security Analyses and Blueberry1vom1t’s Discount Paint Plugin
I have included Blueberry1vom1t’s “Discount Paint Plugin,” which you can toggle on or off, allowing you to change the swizzle of your ship via the job board. I have also included jobs in nearly every populated planet that allow to see your armament deterrance, cargo attraction, and local reputation, which changes based on what I think makes the most sense, but is usually the planetary government or is mentioned in the job description. The main exceptions are the Avgi systems, which are dependent on hidden planet attributes that determine which faction the planet is likely to be aligned with, however, landing on a non tangled shroud planet will get you the generic Avgi reputation, while landing on a twilight guard or dissonance planet will get you twilight guard or angry dissonance reputation. Landing on Weledos will give you the Avgi consonance reputation, and landing on any other Consonance planet in the tangled shroud will get you the Avgi dissonance reputation.

## Uninstalling
Most changes made by this plugin should be automatically reverted when the plugin is uninstalled or disabled. One exception is the Main Plot Skip plotline, where if you uninstall or disable the plugin while in the middle of that plotline, you will be unable to finish the main plot, Hai Reveal, or Wanderers. If you have not finished Main Plot Skip, you will probably have to revert your save, as Wanderers normally requires you to complete Free Worlds. I do not recommend uninstalling or disabling Main Plot Plus if you are in the middle of the Main Plot Skip. You will know when Main Plot Skip is done once you’ve assisted Danforth in taking out pirates, AFTER the main plot has been completed, as this is when Wanderers is unlocked. The other change is that systems that are hidden will continue to be hidden even after the main plot is completed, if you uninstall this plugin prematurely, making it impossible to access most alien systems. The solution here is to temporarily reinstall the plugin once you’ve finished the main plot to unhide these systems. All you have to do is takeoff and land once you’ve done this, and you are good to go.

## Other Plugins and Forks
This plugin is designed for Endless Sky version 0.11.2 or later. Endless Sky Delta is on version 0.10.12 and will not be receiving updates, so it is not supported. My plugin takes the Crisis in Management plugin into account as much as possible. It also used to contain a patch to prevent the Constellations plugin from softlocking the game, but recent versions of this plugin no longer have the issue, so the patch has been removed, and loading this plugin should result in the patch also being reverted. This is because the patch would remove the wormhole if the plugin gets disabled/uninstalled, which I think is worse than old versions of one plugin breaking. Furthermore, all global conditions used by Main Plot Plus are prefixed with “main plot plus.” Some plugins may add in modded systems. Only a limited list of these plugins is supported for “Hidden Sky.” Check above to see if your plugin is supported. If it isn’t, I recommend that you choose the vanilla+ preset and send a ping to me on Discord.

### Mega Freight Battles
Mega Freight Battles conflicts with Main Plot Plus. This was something I was initially working around, however, Saugia has nerfed the Free Worlds battles and especially made the fleets in those battles smaller. Therefore, I have nerfed my versions of the battles, and opted not to support Mega Freight Battles. This is because Mega Freight Battles makes the fleets even bigger, which is contrary to Saugia’s goal of reducing fleet size, and therefore is not something I will be supporting any longer. If you have Mega Freight Battles, you CAN use this plugin, however, the following missions may be one or the other due to conflicts and race conditions:

Conflicting missions:
```
FW Defend New Tibet (patched)
FW Liberate Delta Sagitarii (patched)
FWC Attack Kaus Borealis (patched)
```

I have made a full release, which means that there will be no more “breaking” changes. For those who just downloaded the beta or full release, I still make no guarantees, but I will try to prevent save issues from now on. Note that I still may rename missions, for instance, but I will provide patch missions, whereas before this wasn't the case. However, those playing before the release will likely have to go through breaking changes when updating to a release branch, including, but not limited to, renaming the plugin, renaming files, removing files, reorganizing files, splitting files, merging files, and modifying data files without providing patch missions or other backwards compatibility features. For those who tested out my plugin before release, thank you, but treat this like you would the “continuous” branch of Endless Sky, except for updates after the release, and do not be surprised if you end up with broken saves.
